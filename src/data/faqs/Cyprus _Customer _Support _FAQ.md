Cyprus Customer Support FAQ

(For Private Universities & Colleges such as UCLan Cyprus, University of Nicosia, American College, City Unity College, CDA College, Mesoyios College, Philips University, etc.)

1. Admission Related Questions
Q: Is it possible to get admission with my GPA/CGPA?

Answer: Generally, for Bachelor's programs, a minimum GPA of 2.50+ in SSC & HSC is required, and for Master's programs, a CGPA of 2.50+/4.00 is acceptable. Final eligibility can be confirmed after profile evaluation.

Q: Which university is best for my profile?

Answer: Suitable universities/colleges will be recommended based on your academic background, study gap, IELTS score, and budget.

Q: Can I get admission without IELTS?

Answer: No. For Cyprus Bachelor's and Master's programs, IELTS is generally required (around 5.5 for Bachelor's and around 6.0/5.5 for Master's). However, in some cases, applicants with lower IELTS scores (such as 4.5–5.0) may apply for Foundation or English Preparatory programs.

Q: Are Duolingo or PTE accepted?

Answer: No, only IELTS is accepted.

Q: What is the application deadline?

Answer: February and September are the most popular intakes in Cyprus. It is advisable to start the application process 3–4 months before the intake.

Q: Can I get admission with a study gap?

Answer: Yes, long study gaps are generally accepted in Cyprus.

Q: Can I change my subject/major?

Answer: Yes, in many cases subject changes are possible. The admission committee makes the final decision based on your academic background.

Q: Is credit transfer possible?

Answer: Some universities may allow credit transfers. The final decision is made after reviewing previous transcripts.

Q: What is a Conditional Offer Letter?

Answer: A Conditional Offer Letter means that certain documents or requirements must be submitted later to fulfill the admission conditions.

Q: How long does it take to receive an admission decision?

Answer: Generally, offer letters are issued within 3–10 working days.

2. Scholarship Related Questions
Q: Are scholarships available?

Answer: Yes, many universities in Cyprus offer merit-based scholarships.

Q: How much scholarship can I get with my profile?

Answer: Depending on academic results, a scholarship of around €500 may be available.

Q: Is a full scholarship available?

Answer: Generally, full scholarships are not available. However, partial scholarships may be offered.

Q: What are the requirements to maintain a scholarship?

Answer: Students must maintain the required CGPA as specified by the university.

Q: Is scholarship consideration automatic or do I need to apply separately?

Answer: In most cases, scholarship consideration is included with the admission application.

Q: When will I know the scholarship result?

Answer: Scholarship decisions are usually mentioned in the offer letter.

3. Tuition Fee & Financial Questions
Q: What is the total tuition fee?

Answer: Depending on the program and institution, tuition fees generally range from €5,500–€10,000+ per year.

Q: How much needs to be paid per year/semester?

Answer: Payments can usually be made semester-wise or yearly, depending on university policy.

Q: Can tuition fees be paid in installments?

Answer: Generally, tuition fees are not paid in installments. In most cases, 70% of the tuition fee must be paid before the visa application and the remaining 30% after visa approval.

Q: Is there an application fee?

Answer: No, there are no application fees.

Q: What is the estimated living cost?

Answer: Living costs generally range from €400–€800 per month.

Q: How much bank statement is required?

Answer: Generally, a bank statement showing BDT 12–15 lakh is required, and the funds should have a savings history of approximately 3–6 months.

4. Visa Related Questions
Q: What is the visa success rate?

Answer: Almost 100% guarantee.

Q: How long does visa processing take?

Answer: Generally, visa processing may take 2–4 months.

Q: Is there an embassy interview?

Answer: No, generally Cyprus student visas do not require an embassy interview. Most documents are submitted via courier (DHL), and the visa decision is made after document review.

Q: What happens if my visa is refused?

Answer: The refusal reason can be analyzed and there may be an opportunity to reapply.

Q: What is the visa fee?

Answer: Approximately BDT 10,000–15,000 (including visa and VFS fees).

Q: What documents are required for a visa?

Answer: Passport, academic documents, financial documents, accommodation proof, medical report, and other supporting documents are generally required.

Q: Will a previous visa refusal be a problem?

Answer: No, applicants with previous refusals can still apply. However, refusal details must be disclosed.

Q: Who can be a sponsor?

Answer: Parents, siblings, or close relatives can generally act as sponsors.

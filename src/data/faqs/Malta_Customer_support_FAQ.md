# Malta Student FAQ

## 1. Admission Related Questions

### Q: Is it possible to get admission with my GPA/CGPA?

**Answer:** Generally, in Malta, a minimum GPA of 3.00 in SSC & HSC for Bachelor's programs and a CGPA of 2.50/4.00 for Master's programs is required for admission.

### Q: Which institute is best for my profile?

**Answer:** Suitable institutes will be recommended based on your academic background, study gap, IELTS score, and budget.

### Q: Can I get admission without IELTS?

**Answer:** Yes, in Malta some Diploma/PGD programs allow admission without IELTS. However, for Direct Bachelor's and Master's programs, IELTS 5.5 is generally required.

### Q: Are Duolingo and PTE accepted?

**Answer:** No. Currently, Direct Bachelor's and Master's programs require a minimum IELTS score of 5.5. Students without IELTS may first enroll in a 1-year Diploma/Level-4 program and later progress to Bachelor's or higher studies through the progression route.

### Q: What is the application deadline?

**Answer:** Malta generally offers January, March, June, and October intakes.

### Q: Can I get admission with a study gap?

**Answer:** Yes, study gaps are accepted in Malta.

* Bachelor's: Generally pass-outs up to 2018 are accepted.
* Master's: Generally applicants up to 27 years of age are accepted.
* Some institutes also accept longer study gaps.

### Q: Can I change my subject?

**Answer:** Yes, subject changes are possible in many cases. The final decision is made by the institute admission team.

### Q: What is a Conditional Offer Letter?

**Answer:** A Conditional Offer Letter is an offer where certain documents or requirements must be submitted later.

### Q: How long does it take to receive an admission decision?

**Answer:** Generally, admission decisions are provided within 3–5 working days.

## 2. Scholarship Related Questions

### Q: Are scholarships available?

**Answer:** Scholarships in Malta are very limited. Some institutes provide merit-based scholarships.

### Q: How much scholarship can I get with my profile?

**Answer:** Eligible students in the GBS Malta MBA program may receive up to 30% scholarship.

### Q: What are the minimum requirements for a scholarship?

**Answer:** Generally:

* CGPA 3.50+/4.00
* IELTS 6.0–6.5
* Strong academic profile

---

## 3. Tuition Fee & Financial Questions

### Q: What is the total tuition fee?

**Answer:** Depending on the program, tuition fees generally range from €6,500–€10,000.

### Q: Can tuition fees be paid semester-wise or must they be paid yearly?

**Answer:** Generally, after visa approval, the full one-year tuition fee must be paid.

### Q: Do tuition fees need to be paid before the visa?

**Answer:** Generally, tuition fees in Malta are paid after visa approval (except for GBS Malta).

### Q: What is the application fee?

**Answer:** Generally, there is no application fee in Malta. However, some institutes may charge a Registration Fee, CVU Fee, or Admission Processing Fee, which varies by institution.

### Q: What is the CVU Fee?

**Answer:** The CVU (Central Visa Unit) Fee is €150 and is non-refundable.

In Bangladeshi Taka, it is approximately BDT 25,000 (subject to exchange rate fluctuations).

### Q: What is the estimated living cost?

**Answer:** The average living cost in Malta is approximately €400–€700 per month.

### Q: How much bank statement is required?

**Answer:** Generally, applicants need to show funds equivalent to approximately BDT 20 lakh in a savings account with a minimum maturity period of 6 months.

## 4. Visa Related Questions

### Q: What is the visa success rate?

**Answer:** Visa approval depends on the embassy's decision. Applicants with complete documents and genuine profiles generally have a good chance of approval.

### Q: How long does visa processing take?

**Answer:** Generally, the embassy may take approximately 8 weeks to provide a decision.

### Q: Is there an embassy interview?

**Answer:** Yes, interviews may take place at VFS or online.

### Q: What is the visa fee?

**Answer:** VFS and visa submission fees are generally around BDT 25,000.

### Q: What documents are required for a visa?

**Answer:** Passport, academic documents, bank statements, sponsor documents, accommodation proof, insurance, SOP, flight booking, and other supporting documents are generally required.

### Q: Will a previous visa refusal be a problem?

**Answer:** No, applicants can still apply after a previous refusal. The refusal letter must be submitted.

### Q: Who can be a sponsor?

**Answer:** A sponsor can be the father or mother. If the father is deceased, the mother can act as the sponsor. If both parents are unavailable, an elder brother may act as the sponsor.

## 5. English Language Requirements

### Q: What IELTS score is required?

**Answer:** Generally, IELTS 5.5 is required for Direct Bachelor's and Master's programs.

### Q: Can I get admission with IELTS 5.5?

**Answer:** Yes, IELTS 5.5 is accepted for most Bachelor's and Master's programs.

### Q: Is admission possible without IELTS?

**Answer:** Yes, admission without IELTS is possible at Diploma/PGD level. However, Direct Bachelor's and Master's programs generally require IELTS.

### Q: Is MOI accepted?

**Answer:** No, MOI (Medium of Instruction) is not accepted.

For Direct Bachelor's and Master's programs in Malta, IELTS 5.5 is mandatory. Students without IELTS must first enroll in a 1-year Diploma/Level-4 program.

### Q: What should I do if I do not have IELTS?

**Answer:** You must first complete a 1-year Diploma/Level-4 program. After that, progression to Bachelor's or higher studies is possible.

## 7. Work Opportunity Questions

### Q: Can students work part-time?

**Answer:** Yes, international students in Malta can work up to 20 hours per week.

### Q: Is it easy to get a job?

**Answer:** Job availability depends on the student's skills, experience, and English proficiency.

### Q: Is it possible to pay tuition fees by working?

**Answer:** Students can work up to 20 hours per week on a student visa.

This helps manage living expenses, but students should have financial preparation for tuition fees in advance. During summer, full-time work may help cover part of the tuition fees in some cases.

---

## 8. Post-Study & PR Questions

### Q: Can I get a work permit after graduation?

**Answer:** Yes, after completing studies, graduates can receive a 9-month work permit.

### Q: Is there a pathway to PR?

**Answer:** Yes, after completing 5 years of legal stay, students may become eligible to apply for Permanent Residency (PR).

### Q: Can I stay back after completing my degree?

**Answer:** Yes, eligible graduates may stay through the work permit route.

### Q: Can I extend my residence permit after getting a job?

**Answer:** Yes, residence permits can be renewed or extended based on employment.

---

## 9. Country-Specific Questions

### Q: Is Malta a Schengen country?

**Answer:** Yes, Malta is a European Schengen country.

### Q: What is the capital city?

**Answer:** Valletta is the capital city of Malta.

### Q: How many intakes are available in Malta?

**Answer:** Generally, February, June, and October intakes are available. Depending on the institute, January and March intakes may also be available.

### Q: Where should the visa application be submitted?

**Answer:** Visa applications must be submitted through VFS Dhaka, Bangladesh.

### Q: How long does it take to get a VFS appointment?

**Answer:** Currently, obtaining a VFS appointment may take some time because appointment slots are limited. At present, appointments are generally being issued for the January intake.

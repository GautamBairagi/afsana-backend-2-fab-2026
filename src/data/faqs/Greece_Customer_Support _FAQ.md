# Greece Student FAQ (Bachelor, Foundation & Master's)

## 1. Admission Related Questions

### Q: Is it possible to get admission with my GPA/CGPA?

**Answer:** Generally, for Bachelor's programs, a minimum GPA of 2.50–3.00 in SSC & HSC and for Master's programs, a CGPA of 2.50+/4.00 may make admission possible. Final eligibility will be confirmed after profile evaluation.

### Q: Which university/college is best for my profile?

**Answer:** Suitable institutions will be recommended based on your academic background, IELTS score, study gap, and budget.

### Q: Can I get admission without IELTS?

**Answer:** For Foundation Programmes, admission is possible with IELTS 4.5 or MOI (Medium of Instruction) or another accepted English proficiency certificate.

For Direct Bachelor’s and Master’s Programmes, IELTS is not mandatory, but some form of English proficiency proof is required, such as:

* IELTS
* PTE
* TOEFL iBT
* Duolingo
* MOI (Medium of Instruction)

Admission is granted subject to meeting the institution’s language requirements.

### Q: Are Duolingo and PTE accepted?

**Answer:** Yes, Duolingo, PTE, and TOEFL are accepted by Greece partner institutions.

### Q: What is the application deadline?

**Answer:** Greece generally has two intakes:

* September Intake
* January Intake

It is recommended to start the application process at least 4–5 months before the intake to allow enough time for admission, visa processing, and document preparation.

### Q: Can I get admission with a study gap?

**Answer:** Yes, study gaps are accepted.

### Q: Can I change my subject?

**Answer:** Yes, subject changes may be possible in many cases. Eligibility is assessed according to academic background.

### Q: Is credit transfer possible?

**Answer:** No, credit transfer opportunities are generally not available. Students usually need to start a new programme, and previous credits cannot normally be transferred.

### Q: What is a Conditional Offer Letter?

**Answer:** A Conditional Offer Letter is an offer where certain documents or requirements must be submitted later.

### Q: How long does it take to receive an admission decision?

**Answer:** Usually, a decision is made within 1–3 weeks after submitting a complete application.

## 2. Scholarship Related Questions

### Q: Are scholarships available?

**Answer:** No, currently there are no scholarships available at our Greece partner institutions. All students study on a self-funded basis according to the university's tuition fee structure.

### Q: Is a full scholarship available?

**Answer:** Generally, full scholarships are not available for self-funded students.

### Q: What is required to maintain a scholarship?

**Answer:** Good academic performance must be maintained.

## 3. Tuition Fee & Financial Questions

### Q: What is the total tuition fee?

**Answer:**

**Bachelor's:** €6,500 – €8,500

**Master's:** €7,500 – €11,000

### Q: Is there an application fee?

**Answer:** No, there is no application fee.

### Q: Is the registration fee refundable?

**Answer:** After receiving the Conditional Offer Letter, a registration fee of €300–€400 must be paid. This fee is generally non-refundable. If the visa is approved, the registration fee will be deducted from the tuition fee.

### Q: What is the estimated living cost?

**Answer:** Depending on lifestyle and city, living expenses are generally around €400–€600 per month.

### Q: How much bank statement is required?

**Answer:** Students should be prepared to show financial capacity equivalent to approximately 30 lakh BDT. A savings account history of 4–6 months is recommended.

## 4. Visa Related Questions

### Q: What is the visa success rate?

**Answer:** Visa approval depends on the embassy’s decision. Applicants with complete documentation and a genuine profile generally have a good approval chance. The acceptance rate is approximately 85–90%.

### Q: How long does visa processing take?

**Answer:** Depending on embassy workload, visa processing generally takes 2–3 months.

### Q: Is there an embassy interview?

**Answer:** Yes, an embassy interview may be required.

### Q: What happens if my visa is refused?

**Answer:** The refusal reason will be analyzed and the next appropriate steps will be advised.

### Q: What is the visa fee?

**Answer:** The visa fee is €90.

### Q: What documents are required for a visa?

**Answer:** Passport, academic documents, financial documents, accommodation proof, insurance, and other supporting documents are generally required.

### Q: Will a previous visa refusal be a problem?

**Answer:** No, applicants can still apply with a previous refusal. However, refusal details must be disclosed.

### Q: Who can be a sponsor?

**Answer:** Parents, siblings, uncles (father’s brother), or maternal uncles may act as sponsors.

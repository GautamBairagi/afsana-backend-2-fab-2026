Hungary Customer Support FAQ

(For prospective students of Hungarian Universities such as Corvinus, Debrecen, ELTE, University of Pécs, University of Szeged, University of Nyíregyháza, Széchenyi István University (Győr), John von Neumann University, Obuda University, and Pannonia University)

1. Admission Related Questions
Q: Is it possible to get admission with my GPA/CGPA?

Answer: Admission eligibility depends on the university, program, and academic background. Based on your SSC/HSC or Bachelor's results, study gap, and English proficiency, we can suggest suitable universities.

Q: Which university is best for my profile?

Answer: We will recommend the best-fit Hungarian university based on your academic background, GPA, IELTS score, budget, and preferred subject.

Q: Can I get admission without IELTS?

Answer: Some Hungarian universities evaluate English proficiency through MOI (Medium of Instruction) or an online interview. However, requirements may vary depending on the program and university.

Q: Are Duolingo and PTE accepted?

Answer: Some universities accept Duolingo or PTE, but IELTS is the most widely accepted. We can check university-specific requirements for you.

Q: What is the application deadline?

Answer: Deadlines vary by university and intake. For the September intake, applications generally start several months earlier. Most universities close applications by March, although some continue accepting applications until June.

Q: Can I get admission with a study gap?

Answer: Yes, admission is possible with a reasonable study gap. Gap justification and supporting documents may be required. Generally, Hungarian universities accept up to 4 years of study gap for Bachelor's programs and up to 7–8 years for Master's programs. Proper explanation and supporting documents regarding employment, business activities, training, or other activities during the gap period may be required.

Q: Can I change my subject?

Answer: In many cases, subject changes are possible. However, the university admission committee will evaluate your academic background.

Q: Is credit transfer possible?

Answer: Some universities accept credit transfers. The decision is made after reviewing previous transcripts and course syllabi.

Q: What is a Conditional Offer Letter?

Answer: A Conditional Offer Letter is an admission offer where certain requirements (such as final certificates, IELTS results, or tuition payment) must be fulfilled.

Q: How long does it take to receive an admission decision?

Answer: Generally, a decision is received within 2–8 weeks after submitting a complete application.

2. Scholarship Related Questions
Q: Are scholarships available?

Answer: Some Hungarian universities offer merit-based scholarships. Eligibility depends on academic performance.

Q: How much scholarship can I get with my profile?

Answer: Scholarship amounts depend on GPA, academic achievements, and university policies. An estimated scholarship amount can be provided after profile evaluation.

Q: What are the minimum requirements for a scholarship?

Answer: For scholarship consideration, most Hungarian universities generally require:

SSC/HSC GPA: 4.00+
Bachelor CGPA: 3.00+/4.00 (for Master's applicants)
IELTS: 5.5–6.0+
Strong academic profile
Q: Is a full scholarship available?

Answer: Full scholarships for self-funded students are very limited. However, the Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship Program is available separately.

3. Tuition Fee & Financial Questions
Q: What is the total tuition fee?

Answer: Depending on the program and university, tuition fees generally range from €2,500–€8,000+ per year.

Q: How much needs to be paid per year/semester?

Answer: Most universities allow semester-wise payment. The exact amount is mentioned in the university offer letter.

Q: Should tuition fees be paid for one semester or one year?

Answer: For our partner Hungarian universities, one year’s tuition fee generally needs to be paid in advance for visa processing purposes. Payment must be made according to the university's official instructions.

Q: What is the application fee?

Answer: Application fees vary by university. Generally, Hungarian universities charge around €100–€200 as an application fee.

Q: Is the registration fee refundable?

Answer: Registration and application fees are generally non-refundable, although policies may vary by university.

Q: What is the estimated living cost?

Answer: The average living cost in Hungary is approximately €400–€600 per month, depending on lifestyle and city.

4. Bank & Sponsor Related Questions
Q: How much bank statement is required?

Answer: For a Hungary student visa, applicants generally need to show approximately BDT 25 lakh (or equivalent) in a bank statement. The funds should remain in a savings account for at least 12–13 months and serve as proof of financial support for tuition and living expenses.

Q: Who can be a sponsor?

Answer: A sponsor can be the father or mother. If the father is deceased, the mother can be the sponsor. If both parents are unavailable, an elder brother may act as the sponsor.

Q: Do you provide banking and document support?

Answer: Yes, we provide support regarding banking and required documentation. However, additional service charges may apply.

Q: What sponsor documents are required?

Answer: Generally, the following documents are required:

National ID Card (NID) or Passport
Bank Statements (last 6–12 months)
Income Certificate / Job Letter / Salary Certificate
Tax Return Certificate (TIN / ITR)
Trade License (if applicable)
Bank Solvency Certificate
Relationship Proof (Birth Certificate / Family Certificate)
5. Visa Related Questions
Q: What is the visa success rate?

Answer: Visa approval depends on the embassy’s decision. Applicants with complete documentation and genuine student profiles generally have a good chance of approval.

Q: How long does visa processing take?

Answer: Visa processing generally takes 15–60 days, depending on the season.

Q: Is there an embassy interview?

Answer: Yes, the interview process is generally completed through VFS.

Q: What happens if my visa is refused?

Answer: The refusal reasons can be analyzed, and options for reapplication or appeal can be discussed.

Q: What is the visa fee?

Answer: Visa fees vary according to the VFS package selected. Typically, the cost ranges between BDT 20,000–25,000 depending on whether it is a Silver, Golden, or Premium package.

Q: What documents are required for a visa?

Answer: Passport, admission letter, accommodation proof, financial documents, insurance, academic documents, and other supporting documents are generally required.

Q: Will a previous visa refusal be a problem?

Answer: No, applicants can still apply after a previous refusal. However, the refusal reasons should be properly addressed.

6. English Language Requirements
Q: What IELTS score is required?

Answer: Most English-taught programs require IELTS scores between 5.5–6.5, depending on the program.

Q: Can I get admission with IELTS 5.0/5.5/6.0?

Answer: Some universities accept IELTS 5.5, while others may require 6.0 or higher.

Q: Is admission possible without IELTS?

Answer: Some universities accept MOI or interviews instead of IELTS. University-specific policies must be checked.

Q: Is MOI accepted?

Answer: Some Hungarian universities accept MOI, but acceptance varies depending on the program.

Q: Are English preparatory courses available?

Answer: Yes, some universities offer English preparatory or foundation programs.

7. Accommodation Questions
Q: Is university dormitory accommodation available?

Answer: Yes, most Hungarian universities provide dormitory accommodation, subject to availability.

Q: What is the accommodation cost?

Answer: Dormitories generally cost €100–€300 per month, while private accommodation may cost €250–€600+ per month.

Q: Is airport pickup available?

Answer: Some universities or student organizations provide airport pickup support.

Q: Does the agency assist with accommodation booking?

Answer: Yes, guidance and support are provided throughout the accommodation booking process.

8. Work Opportunity Questions
Q: Is it possible to pay tuition fees by working?

Answer: Yes, international students in Hungary can work up to 30 hours per week during studies and full-time during summer breaks. With proper planning and savings, students may be able to manage living expenses and potentially contribute toward tuition fees.

Q: Can students work part-time?

Answer: Yes, students with a valid residence permit may work part-time according to Hungarian regulations.

Q: How many hours can students work per week?

Answer: International students can work up to 30 hours per week and full-time during summer vacations.

Q: Is it easy to find a job?

Answer: Job opportunities depend on the city, language skills, experience, and labor market demand.

Q: What is the average salary?

Answer: Student job salaries vary depending on the position and employer.

Q: Can I get a job with only English language skills?

Answer: Some international positions require only English, but knowing Hungarian increases job opportunities significantly.

9. Post-Study & PR Questions
Q: Can I get a work permit after graduation?

Answer: Yes, graduates who receive a job offer may apply for the relevant residence or work permit.

Q: How long is the PSW (Post Study Work) period?

Answer: Generally, a 9-month Post Study Work (PSW) period is available.

Q: Is there a pathway to PR?

Answer: Future PR eligibility may be possible after meeting long-term legal stay and immigration requirements.

Q: Can I stay back after completing my degree?

Answer: Job-search and employment-related residence options may be available after graduation.

Q: Can I extend my residence permit after getting a job?

Answer: Yes, residence permit conversion or extension may be possible under eligible employment categories.
